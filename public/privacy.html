Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-10

Who we are

Otto is operated by Airlock Labs ("we," "us," or "our"). We build autonomous AI agents for community management. You can reach us about anything in this policy at privacy@orbitwithotto.com.

Information we collect

We collect the minimum information needed to operate Otto and measure how the marketing site is performing. Specifically:

  • Waitlist signups. When you submit the "Get early access" form on our landing page, we collect the fields you provide: email address (required), name (optional), platform (optional), community size (optional), and referral source.
  • Google Analytics 4 on our marketing pages (/ and /otto). GA4 sets cookies and collects: truncated IP address, device and browser metadata (user agent, screen resolution, language), pages viewed, CTA clicks (e.g. "Start Free", "Join the Waitlist"), and the waitlist submission event.
  • Polsia analytics beacon. A small pixel that sets a pseudonymous visitor identifier (polsia_vid) in your browser's localStorage and fires a single page-view event. No per-user PII is attached to the beacon payload.

How we use information

  • To operate the waitlist and onboard communities in the order they signed up.
  • To send confirmation and onboarding emails (delivered via our email provider; we do not sell or rent your email).
  • To measure aggregate landing-page traffic and CTA performance so we can improve the site.
  • To respond to support requests and rights requests you send us.

Cookies & local storage

  • Google Analytics 4 sets the _ga and _ga_* cookies used to distinguish unique visitors and sessions.
  • Polsia sets the polsia_vid key in your browser's localStorage (not a cookie).

The policy pages themselves do not set analytics cookies or fire tracking beacons.

Retention

  • Waitlist rows are kept until you ask us to remove them, or until your account is onboarded and migrated into the active Otto service.
  • GA4 data is retained per Google's default 14-month retention window.
  • Polsia beacon events are aggregated for traffic measurement and are not retained in a per-user identifiable form beyond the pseudonym in your own browser.

Sharing

We share information only with the processors needed to run Otto:

  • Google — as the operator of Google Analytics 4 for traffic measurement.
  • Polsia — as the operator of the analytics beacon and our email-sending infrastructure.
  • Stripe — only if and when you choose to pay for a paid Otto plan. No payment data is collected on the marketing site today.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with advertising networks.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California, you have the right to:

  • Right to access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to delete the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share for cross-context advertising).
  • Right to data portability for information you have provided to us.

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@orbitwithotto.com. We respond within 30 days.

How to opt out of GA4

You can opt out of Google Analytics 4 tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. Most modern browsers also honor a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal; we respect those where technically feasible.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify active waitlist members by email.
