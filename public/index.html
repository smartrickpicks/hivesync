Five functions. One agent.

Otto's behavior is a constellation of five specialized functions, each tuned for a different dimension of community management.

Scout

Scout

Monitors every incoming message across channels, classifies intent, and surfaces conversations that need a human eye.

Forge

Forge

Drafts and generates on-brand responses tuned to each member's tone, question type, and community context.

Sentinel

Sentinel

Detects spam, enforces community rules, and quietly removes bad actors before they disrupt the conversation.

Relay

Relay

Cross-posts announcements and threads across Discord, Twitter, and forums — keeping every channel in sync.

Pulse

Pulse

Scores community health over time — tracking sentiment, activity, churn risk, and growth momentum in one dashboard.
